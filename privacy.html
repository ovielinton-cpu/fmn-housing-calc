Naira Finance Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · App package: com.linton.naira_finance_hub

This policy explains what information the Naira Finance Hub app ("the app") handles and how. The app is published by Linton ("we", "us"). We built the app so that your financial information stays on your own phone.

1. Information you enter stays on your device

Everything you type or save in the app is stored only on your phone, including:

  • housing upfront and salary/PAYE calculations, and saved salary history;
  • Money Tracker transactions, budgets, categories and Quick Add shortcuts;
  • Ajo group names, members, contributions, payout order and history;
  • your name or nickname used for the greeting.

We do not have servers that receive this information, we never see it, and we do not sell or share it. There are no user accounts and no login.

2. Files you choose to create

When you use Backup, Share Group or Export PDF, the app creates a file and saves it only where you choose (for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive). If you send that file to someone, for example on WhatsApp, you decide who receives it. We never receive a copy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect and share the following with Google:

DataPurpose
Device or other IDs (such as the advertising ID)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (estimated from your IP address)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactions (for example, ad views and taps)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Crash logs and diagnosticsAnalytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent over an encrypted connection. Google's use of it is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some phones).

4. Permissions

The app uses the Internet permission only so that ads can load. It does not read your contacts, messages, photos or precise location.

5. Deleting your data

Because your information is stored only on your phone, you are in full control of it. You can delete individual entries inside the app, delete an Ajo group, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Backup files you saved yourself must be deleted from wherever you saved them. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
